Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Weatherly (the "Application"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

Summary: We do NOT collect or store your personal data on our own servers. Data operations are performed locally or shared with trusted third-party weather providers as selected by you.

2. Data Collection and Usage

2.1 Personal Data

We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email, phone number, or address. We do not maintain any external servers to store user-specific data.

2.2 Location Data

Optionally, GlobalWeather can access your approximate or precise location to find weather data for your position. This data is shared with the weather sources you select in the Application settings. If you do not want to share your location, you can deny permissions and choose a city manually.

2.3 Icon Packs & Package Visibility

To provide weather icon customization, this Application queries for installed icon packs locally. It only lists packages that match specific weather icon pack provider signatures. No package data is collected, uploaded, or shared.

2.4 App Updates

If enabled, the Application connects to GitHub to check for updates. Your version number is compared on-device; no personal info is transmitted during this check.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Since we do not collect or store personal data on any servers, we do not retain your data. Any location data used to fetch weather is processed in real-time and not stored by the Application. You can stop all data processing simply by uninstalling the Application or disabling location permissions in your device settings.

4. Third-Party Services

GlobalWeather relies on third-party APIs for weather data and geocoding. These providers have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them via the "Privacy Policy" link in the Application's settings menu. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs or sell any user data.

Permission Usage Documentation

1. Location Permissions

  • android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Required to obtain your position to fetch relevant weather data.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION: Necessary for background refreshes to keep widgets and notifications up-to-date.

2. Storage Permissions

  • android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used for reading wallpaper/theme data during Application configuration (legacy support for Android versions below 13).

3. Network and Connectivity

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to communicate with weather data providers.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE & ACCESS_WIFI_STATE: Used to determine if a data connection is available before attempting updates.

4. System and Background Features

  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC: Ensures that background weather updates are completed reliably.
  • android.permission.REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS: Prevents the system from delaying critical weather alerts and widget refreshes.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to display weather alerts and precipitation notifications.
  • android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Automatically resumes weather update schedules after a device restart.
  • com.android.alarm.permission.SET_ALARM: Used for precise scheduling of weather data synchronization.
